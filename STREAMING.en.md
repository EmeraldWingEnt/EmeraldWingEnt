## Tools I built for it

I approach the streaming/community side the same way I approach development: where the right tool didn't exist, I built it.

- **Twitch schedule overlay** — a custom-built Twitch Extension, built on the Twitch Helix API, that shows viewers the stream schedule.
- **Discord community bot** — a custom bot handling moderation, Twitch integration, and notifications for the community.
