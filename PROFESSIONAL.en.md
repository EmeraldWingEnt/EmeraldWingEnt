# EmeraldWing Entertainment

*[🇭🇺 Magyar verzió](./PROFESSIONAL.hu.md)*

Most of my projects start from the same reflex: if a tool is missing or isn't good enough, I build it. That's what took me from systems programming through AI/LLM infrastructure to my own game engine — as an independent software architect and systems engineer. EmeraldWing Entertainment is my solo-led studio, where I build my own tools, game technology, and content platforms from the ground up.

One highlight: a from-scratch game engine with a full C++ AI inference and training engine built into it — the latter now powers several of my other systems.

## What I do

- **Systems & low-level engineering** — C++ systems programming, custom engine architecture, performance-critical code.
- **AI/LLM engineering** — local inference runtimes, fine-tuning pipelines, and AI-based tooling built in-house rather than wrapped around third-party APIs.
- **Full-stack development** — desktop (Electron, Tauri), web (React/TypeScript), and backend systems, with a strong focus on reliability.
- **Developer tooling & internal infrastructure** — custom middleware, i18n systems, editor extensions, whenever the available tools aren't enough or don't exist.
- **Game development** — original engine work and game projects, currently including an exploration-driven FPS RPG and an arcade-style maze racing game.
- **Automation & community tooling** — Discord bot development, workflow automation, and platform integrations supporting a multi-channel content operation ([details →](./STREAMING.en.md)).
- **Documentation & technical communication** — architecture write-ups, onboarding guides, coding conventions — a codebase that's not just functional, but followable.
